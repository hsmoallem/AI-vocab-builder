# Building a Better Vocab App: Why My App's SRS Beats Anki for Language Learning

I recently dug into the underlying spaced repetition algorithms powering my new vocabulary app, and I wanted to share a quick technical breakdown comparing it to the industry giant: Anki.

Here is the breakdown of how my app's Spaced Repetition System (SRS) compares to Anki:

### 1. The Core Algorithm (Tie)
My app uses SM-2 (SuperMemo-2), which is the exact same underlying mathematical algorithm that Anki built its entire empire on. The math that calculates "Ease Factors" and scales intervals out from 6 days to 14 days to 30 days is fundamentally the same.

### 2. Intra-day Learning Steps (Anki Wins)
Anki has a complex "Learning Phase." When you see a new card in Anki, you might have to hit "Good" 3 or 4 times (e.g., 1 min, 10 min, 1 hour steps) before it officially "graduates" to a 1-day interval. 
My app intentionally skips this complexity. If you hit "Again", it stays in the queue for today. If you hit "Good" or "Easy", it instantly graduates to a 2 or 4-day interval. It's much faster to use, though Anki is admittedly better if you are trying to rote-memorize incredibly difficult words that you forget every 10 minutes.

### 3. Practical Usage & Engagement (My App Wins)
This is where my app completely changes the game. 
Anki exists in a vacuum. If a word is due in Anki, your only option is to stare at a flashcard.
In my app, the SRS Engine powers everything. If 10 words are mathematically "due" today, you don't *have* to just flip flashcards. You can click a button and have the AI write a custom creative short story using your exact "Due" words, or generate an interactive quiz testing you on those specific terms. 

### Summary
If you are a medical student trying to memorize 10,000 anatomy terms for an exam tomorrow, Anki's complex minute-by-minute steps are better. 

But for **Language Learning**, where seeing words naturally in context (sentences, stories, quizzes) is infinitely more valuable than rote memorization, my app is vastly superior because the SM-2 algorithm actively feeds directly into an AI story generator.
